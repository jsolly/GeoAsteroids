## Contour travel

Follow the neutral gray contours in either direction for extra speed, most where lines are packed tightly and little where they are widely spaced; diagonal travel earns less, while perpendicular crossings and empty space keep normal cruise. Curved streamlines trail behind your ship as the terrain bonus builds, then fade as you turn across the grain. Ordinary terrain travel causes no damage, sideways pull, or shot deflection.

Tap Contour Lock near a visible line to catch it and follow its curves at stronger speed, including shallow bends where the ordinary terrain bonus fades. Steering is ignored until you tap the control again, take damage, hit the wall or an asteroid (even while protected), are shoved hard, or open a menu; see [Controls](/wiki/#controls) for the full list. Lasers pass through invulnerable hulls. Locking has no charge or cooldown. [Flick or use the bracket keys to catch an adjacent line](/wiki/#controls) when it is within reach.

## Cooperative hops

Make up to three adjacent hops in a quick rhythm to leave visible connecting trails; the third creates a bright crew bridge. A teammate following the same line and direction automatically crosses that bridge and gets a brief burst of contour speed, refreshing the bridge for another teammate. Each pilot can use a bridge once; bridges fade quickly, hops never refill tool cooldowns, and a short pause starts a new combo.

## Navigation crystals

Some rocks marked by pale curved strokes glide along contours. Break one with lasers, or [tap crystal](/wiki/#hauler), to collect a navigation crystal that briefly widens Contour Lock pickup reach. The effect refreshes rather than stacks, leaves hop reach and ordinary speed unchanged, and ends on death.

## Outer boundary

The circular wall destroys a vulnerable ship on contact. Rocks bounce inward, and reflected lasers become dangerous ricochets.

## Ricochet Court

Northeast of Town Square, cyan court panels reflect lasers but let ships pass through. See [bank-shot duels](/wiki/#combat-survival) for the rules.

## Spider nests

Each new nest has a fixed guard pack and mixed salvage, often including [rare equipment](/wiki/#loot-growth); guard and cache numbers are in the reference below. Caches are time-limited, subject to the shared pickup limit, and do not refill when you return. Red map webs mark discovered guarded resources; the mark disappears when the last guard dies or the resource is collected or moved.

## Guard patrols

Guards chase briefly and return home, while rare roaming hunters pursue farther. Spiders come in small, medium, and large bodies with varied legs and markings; larger ones are easier to hit. A bright red wash on nearby lines marks spiders. A red screen edge means one is hunting you. Killing guards clears that nest for the server session, but collecting its resource does not remove surviving guards.

## Survive a hunt

Spiders prefer the most loaded eligible pilot, then the nearest pilot. Furnace refuge, Mineral Scan and guard leashes still limit their hunts.

One bite kills, and one ordinary laser hit kills the spider; otherwise escape its range or reach a lit furnace's safe area; Town Square has a larger sanctuary where spiders will not hunt. Mineral Scan makes spiders in range flee only during its short pulse, then they can return; probes and dark foundations offer no protection.

## Spider tools

Haulers can extract silk or tow a living spider, but the captive can still bite ships within reach, including its Hauler, and towing calls rescuers that can cut the cable. Scouts can attach a probe to follow a guard home to its resource. See the [Hauler](/wiki/#hauler) and [Scout](/wiki/#scout) tools.

## Belt crawlers

The eastern belt also has spiders that cling to rocks and hop between them. Their attacks and tool limits differ from terrain spiders; see [Belt spiders](/wiki/#asteroids).

## Frontier queens

Campaign hive queens have shields: pin one with a Hauler Tow Cable, or coordinate shots from opposite sides to expose her. Living queens regularly birth children while nearby crew keep the encounter awake; defeating a queen permanently clears her hive and exposes its furnace ruin. Ordinary resource nests elsewhere retain their separate patrol rules.

## Timed egg sacs

Destroy every sac around a hive before the countdown from the first pop expires. Failed attempts regrow the sacs; a completed set stays destroyed through travel and restarts. One pilot can complete the challenge.

## Scheduled hazards

Meteor showers and spider swarms alternate on UTC half-hours, with a warning followed by a short active window. They threaten nearby ships and cargo; built furnaces, shared discoveries and campaign progress remain intact. See [network and HUD](/wiki/#hud-network) for the shared countdown.

## Roly-polys

Look for pale green segmented wildlife near the contours. Roly-polys curl when a ship approaches; nudge a curled shell to send it rolling along the slope's contour before it uncurls. They are neutral, cannot hurt your hull, and carry no mining or towing reward.

## Light and wreck shadows

Your headlights illuminate the route ahead, while your engine casts blue light that turns violet at contour speed. Recent wrecks interrupt those beams with ghostly shadows; their broken hull marks fade with the temporary wreck record. The light is shared by nearby pilots and can charge [glowtoys](/wiki/#asteroids).

## Ride the music

Follow the paired stone-like arches through the narrow passages between contour districts. With Music enabled, traveling along a passage gradually brings forward a richer mix of the same track, at the same point in the song. Leaving the route fades back to the quiet mix; threat music still takes priority.
